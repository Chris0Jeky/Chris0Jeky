## What I build

My current projects span developer productivity, creative tooling, developer tooling, browser security, puzzle systems, operational evidence, and engineering research. They share a common approach:

- **local-first where ownership matters;**
- **review before consequential automation;**
- **provenance and evidence instead of unexplained output;**
- **visible unknown, degraded, stale, and failed states;**
- **calm interfaces over activity theatre;**
- **tests, release discipline, accessibility, and recovery as product features.**

## Experience and working principles

- Integrated automated security scanning into roughly 72 AWS CI/CD pipelines using Jenkins, Bash, Groovy, Nmap, and versioned expected-port evidence.
- Published multi-agent simulation and game-theory research through the N-person Prisoner’s Dilemma project and SGAI-AI 2025.
- Build products end to end: product direction, architecture, implementation, tests, CI, release evidence, operations, and user experience.
- Prefer designs where people can see what changed, why it changed, what is uncertain, and how to recover.
- Treat local ownership, least authority, honest degradation, accessibility, and durable evidence as engineering constraints rather than marketing language.
